The Problem

Why platforms like Bikroy, Facebook Marketplace & eBay fall short

Haggling Fatigue

Buyers & sellers waste time on endless manual negotiations. Sellers rarely get true market value; buyers face constant friction.

Reserve Sniffing

Dishonest buyers submit tiny incremental bids — $1, $2, $3 — purely to reverse-engineer the seller's secret minimum without ever intending a fair offer.

Handover Fraud

Offline cash-on-delivery relies on verbal agreements only. Sellers face payment disputes; buyers are vulnerable to false non-delivery claims.

Clock Drift

Real-time bidding suffers from client-server time mismatches, causing disputes over bids placed in the final seconds of an auction.

Our Solution

Three interlocking mechanisms that eliminate every pain point

Double-Blind 3-Bid Auction

Buyers get exactly 3 private chances to meet a hidden reserve price. Bids from all buyers are sealed — no shill bidding, no penny-probing possible.

Dual Pricing

Sellers set a public Display Price and a private Secret Reserve. The system automatically matches without exposing the floor to buyers.

Cryptographic Escrow

On a successful bid, both parties receive a unique 6-digit key. The transaction only closes when both keys are verified — mutual, tamper-proof consent.

3. Double-Verification Escrows

Securing the physical face-to-face handover

When a bid is successful, the system generates unique 6-digit cryptographic codes for both the buyer and the seller.

During the meetup, both parties enter their codes into the portal. The transaction is instantly verified and finalized, preventing scams and guaranteeing proof of exchange.

Limitations & What We Learned

Every system has room for growth

Desktop Only

UI is currently optimised for desktop. Mobile responsiveness was deprioritised during the sprint.

Timer Desync

REST-based countdown polling occasionally produces minor desyncs. WebSockets would solve this cleanly.

Single-Item Escrow

The escrow system currently handles one item per physical meeting. Batch transactions are a future target.

Future Scope

Where LastPrice goes next

Customizable Limits

Letting sellers choose the exact number of bids allowed (e.g. 2 to 5 chances) to control transaction velocity.

Buyer Selection

Allowing sellers to manually pick the winning buyer from the successful pool based on reputation or location.

Trust Points

A gamified reputation system awarding points for successful physical handovers to encourage honest behavior.
