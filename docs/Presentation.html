The Problem

Why traditional marketplaces fall short

Haggling Fatigue

Endless text conversations and low-ball offers lead to frustration and high transaction dropout rates.

Reserve Sniffing

Bad actors use incremental penny bids just to expose the seller's secret minimum price.

Handover Fraud

Physical meetups lack proof of exchange, leaving both buyers and sellers vulnerable to scams.

1. The Silent 3-Bid Arena

Fast, private, and decisive bidding

Double-Blind Privacy

Bids are completely vaulted. Buyers cannot see each other's offers, eliminating bidding wars and peer pressure.

Capped Bidding

Every buyer gets exactly 3 chances to meet the seller's secret price. No more endless low-balling.

3. Double-Verification Escrows

Securing the physical face-to-face handover

When a bid is successful, the system generates unique 6-digit cryptographic codes for both the buyer and the seller.

During the meetup, both parties enter their codes into the portal. The transaction is instantly verified and finalized, preventing scams and guaranteeing proof of exchange.

Technical Details: RGB Feedback

Algorithmic proximity tracking and gamified tension feedback

Percentage = (Placed Bid / Secret Reserve Price) * 100

COLD STATE (<70%)

• Trigger: Bid is far below floor.
• Action: UI glows red and shakes.
• Result: Signals buyer to raise bid.

PROXIMITY STATE (70% - 99%)

• Trigger: Bid is within 70% of floor.
• Action: UI pulses yellow.
• Result: Alerts buyer they are hot.

MATCH STATE (>=100%)

• Trigger: Bid breaches reserve price.
• Action: Confetti, lock input.
• Result: Generates escrow codes.

Limitations & What We Learned

Every system has room for growth

Desktop Only

UI is currently optimised for desktop. Mobile responsiveness was deprioritised during the sprint.

Timer Desync

REST-based countdown polling occasionally produces minor desyncs. WebSockets would solve this cleanly.

Single-Item Escrow

The escrow system currently handles one item per physical meeting. Batch transactions are a future target.

Future Scope

Where LastPrice goes next

Customizable Limits

Letting sellers choose the exact number of bids allowed (e.g. 2 to 5 chances) to control transaction velocity.

Buyer Selection

Allowing sellers to manually pick the winning buyer from the successful pool based on reputation or location.

Trust Points

A gamified reputation system awarding points for successful physical handovers to encourage honest behavior.
